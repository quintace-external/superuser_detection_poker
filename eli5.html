Superuser detection · overview

How a superuser gets caught

Five steps turn raw poker hands into a short list of cases for a human expert.

The idea

An honest player guesses the opponent’s cards from the betting. A superuser sees those cards, so he never has to guess.

Five steps

Each step feeds the next, and a human makes the final call. Tap a box to open that step.

The tell

A superuser folds exactly when he is beaten and calls exactly when the bet is a bluff. Nobody guesses that well for long.
