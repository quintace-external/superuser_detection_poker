Step 04 · predicted range

Guessing the opponent’s cards from his bets

We start from every hand he could hold and fade the ones his bets make unlikely.

The guess narrows

Each square is a starting hand, darker when he holds it more often. Each bet fades the hands that rarely bet like that.

After the river raise

After his river raise, 82 of every 100 hands he could hold are two pair or better. Only 10 are pure air.

How it learns

The model counts what the whole field does with each kind of hand in each spot. After every action it updates the guess.
