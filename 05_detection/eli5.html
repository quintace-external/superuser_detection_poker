Step 05 · detection

From numbers to a case

Signals pick the players, suspicious hands build the case, and a human expert decides.

The funnel

Most players never get past the first cut. Only players high on several signals at once have their hands searched.

Five kinds of suspicious hand

Each suspicious hand falls into one of five types and gets a grade. Type R is new: it uses the predicted range from step 04.

The article's case

In the article's case 74 of 718 heads-up hands were suspicious and the last 15 days escalated. All 74 flagged hands stay on the page. The Equity review puts first the 20 hands where the betting said one thing and he followed the opponent's real cards instead, 3 of them blatant, and ranks low the 37 where the range agrees with him: ordinary play. Knowing the opponents' real cards explains his folds better than for 93% of field players measured on as many responses.

Three ways to cheat, three checks

A superuser may see the whole table, target one victim, or start cheating later. Each case gets its own check.
