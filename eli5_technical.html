Data pipeline map

What each step reads, what it writes to the warehouse (DuckDB, plain SQL), and the columns the next step needs.

00raw input
Text hand histories
PokerStars-style export of any room
01_action_table/run.py
Operator JSON replays
one row per hand, internal format
01_action_table/from_replay_json.py → same loader
Gate: a hand is dropped whole if its money does not reconcile to the cent or a line cannot be read; every reason is counted.
01action table
replay of every hand → three tables at three grains
hands
1 row per hand
blinds, ante, button, board, pot per street, final pot, rake, showdown
hand_players
1 row per player in a hand
seat, position, stack, hole cards, vpip, pfr, saw flop, net_bb
actions
1 row per post or action
street, action, amount, pot_before, to_call, pot_odds, eff_stack, size_pot, faced_size_pot, situation, is_ip, made_hand, draw, board_at_action
20 SQL invariants run after every load (pot and stack accounting, money identity, no action after a fold).
02equity
02_equity/run.py · every postflop decision with known cards
action_equity
1 row per decision
eq_vs_random (vs all hands), eq_vs_real (vs the opponent's real cards), ahead_now
hand_equity
heads-up hands
preflop equity vs the opponent's hand
Exact on turn and river; Monte Carlo with a seed on the flop.
03player stats
03_player_stats/run.py
player_stats
1 row per player
16 classic stats (bb/100, VPIP, PFR, 3-bet, c-bet, WTSD, W$SD, ...) + 9 hidden-information signals (oracle fold ratios, bluff index, fold vs value / bluff, gap, river accuracy)
04predicted range
04_predicted_range/run.py · fit on the field, then score each heads-up decision
rng_prior_pre
field aggregation
hands the field plays per preflop situation × position
rng_lik_post
field aggregation, 6 back-off levels
P(action | street, situation, in/out of position, pot type, made hand, draw)
rng_player_tilt
per player
VPIP, PFR, aggression → widen or narrow his range
action_range_equity
1 row per decision
eq_vs_range, effective combos of the opponent's range
fit on a full-information heads-up population kept outside the repository; bet size enters the likelihood in five tiers. On observer data (showdowns only) the ranges come out too strong.
05detection
05_detection/run.py --player X [--opponent Y]
signals
per player or pair
the 9 signals on the hands in scope
suspicious hands
categories A–D (+R)
label Perfect / Good / Average, snap flag, the three equities, pot odds, range verdict
escalation
last 15 days
flagged share > 5% and > 5 hands
report
for the expert
report.md, CSVs, suspicious_hands.html: every flagged hand ranked by the Equity review, with the opponent's predicted range
